## About me

I build NLP and LLM systems for healthcare in Spanish, with a focus on Chilean clinical data and the regulatory frame (Norma Técnica N°241 MINSAL, Ley 21.719). Co-founded [HealthTracker Analytics](https://healthtracker.ai) in 2021 and lead engineering for the platform that automates clinical billing and reporting flows for major Chilean private health providers. In parallel I teach NLP (INFB6093 — Procesamiento de Lenguaje Natural) at Universidad Tecnológica Metropolitana (UTEM).

Background: Biomedical Engineering + Electrical Engineering (UdeC) + AI (PUC Chile). I work at the intersection of clinical reality, technical rigor, and regulation — the niche I care about most.

I'm from Tomé, Chile — *cuna de Cecilia la incomparable*. I live with my partner and our fur baby, Hiro. I love stories, science fiction, and board games.

I watched us teach machines to understand natural language — and I've watched us actually pull it off. I'm anxious and excited about what's now possible. I believe in educating ourselves, learning, being creative. Staying human.
